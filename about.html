About Reg&Gil

Your Event Architect

We are storytellers, brand strategists and event professionals passionate about crafting your brand stories into impactful events that inspire brand loyalty.

We don't just manage events; we craft unforgettable moments that bring your brand and organisation to life. We help you translate your brand vision into powerful narratives, using strategic communication to amplify your message before, during, and after your event. Our expertise goes beyond execution, we identify gaps that can be bridged through thoughtfully curated experiences.

Founded on the principles of professionalism, proactive problem solving, creativity, and attention to detail, we have delivered exceptional results for clients across sectors in Africa.

Our reach extends beyond borders with Nigerian headquarters in Abuja and regional operations in Lagos, Port-Harcourt and an international presence in Ghana with headquarters in Accra and regional operation in Takoradi.

Business Approach
Conceive

We partner with you to bring your event vision to life. Through brainstorming sessions, we'll develop overarching ideas and strategies that will be crafted into a memorable event that aligns with your goals and target audience.

01

Strategize

Our meticulous strategic approach maps out every logistical and operational detail, from budgeting, venue & vendor selection to risk assessment and contingency planning. Our experts troubleshoot potential challenges and design activations to boost engagement.

02

Execute

As your on-site partners, we take care of everything on event day by seamlessly managing all the logistical aspects including Effortless Setup and Breakdown, Vendor Management, Streamlined Registrations, On-Site Expertise, Event Coordination & Production.

03

Monitor and Evaluate

Beyond logistics, we deliver strategic insights by using our cutting-edge platform to gather attendee feedback, media, and financial data. We then analyse these post-event data and combine it with our event experience to help you optimise future events.
